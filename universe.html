Harmonics in the Universe

Everything Oscillates

From the strings of a violin to the plasma of the early universe, from a beating heart to a pulsar a thousand light-years away — the universe is full of things that oscillate. Once you learn to recognize this one pattern, it shows up almost everywhere: in why a guitar sounds like a guitar, in how astronomers figure out what's inside a star they'll never touch, and in a surprising number of places in between.
